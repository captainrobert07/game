How to play

  • 🪵 Chop trees — click a tree to harvest wood.
  • 🏗 Build — pick a building below, then click an empty grass tile.
  • 🌾 Farms produce food each day.
  • 🏠 Houses attract a new villager (1 per house, max).
  • 🖱 Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
  • 💾 Saves to your browser. No accounts, ever.
